# Project Proposal

**Problem Statement:**  
Manual inspection of car exteriors for damage is time-consuming and subjective.

**Target Industry/Domain:**  
Automotive (car rental, insurance, used car sales)

**Proposed Computer Vision Solution:**  
Automated instance segmentation to detect and highlight damages (scratches, dents) on car images using YOLOv8.

**Expected Impact:**  
- Faster, more objective inspections
- Reduced labor costs
- Improved customer trust

**Deadline:** 13/06/2025
